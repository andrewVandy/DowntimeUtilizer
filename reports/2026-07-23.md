## Ideas discovered

- The single biggest reusable insight from this pass: whenever a
  monetization pattern found in research requires the routine itself to
  contact real third parties (cold outreach, sales calls), the right move
  is to reframe it as a self-serve *product* a human buyer then takes to
  market themselves, rather than trying to make the pattern fit inside
  `POLICY.md`'s guardrails. Both new items reuse this reframe explicitly.
- `WebSearch` query phrasing that mentions a platform by name in plain
  text (e.g. "x.com twitter thread ...") works meaningfully better for
  finding primary social content than the `allowed_domains` parameter,
  which some platforms (Reddit) reject outright at the API level. Worth
  remembering for any future research item in this repo that needs
  social-platform coverage.
- Many individual marketing/SEO blogs return HTTP 403 to `WebFetch` even
  though they appear in `WebSearch` results — for those domains,
  `WebSearch`'s own generated summary is the only usable signal this
  project's tools can currently get, not a direct fetch.
